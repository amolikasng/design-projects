# Little Tokyo — Brand Identity

A ramen restaurant identity inspired by Japanese food culture, bold flavours, and contemporary visual expression.

---

###  About The Project

Little Tokyo is a ramen restaurant concept that brings together the warmth of Japanese food culture and the experience of casual dining. The brand aims to create a distinct visual identity that feels inviting, memorable, and full of character.

---

###  Visual Direction

The identity explores a bold visual language inspired by Japanese aesthetics, food culture, and contemporary design.

Through expressive typography, considered color palettes, and supporting graphic elements, the branding creates a recognizable personality while capturing the vibrant spirit of Little Tokyo.
