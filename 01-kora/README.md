# Kōra — Brand Identity

A skincare brand inspired by nature, simplicity and everyday wellness.

---

###  The Brand

Kōra is built around the idea of returning to natural, plant-based care. The brand focuses on clean formulations, hydration and everyday skin wellness, making skincare feel simple, gentle and intentional.

---

###   The Visual Direction

The identity takes a minimal, soft and understated approach, drawing inspiration from earthy elements and the natural world. Through a considered visual language, the branding reflects Kōra's gentle and honest personality while creating a sense of calm and simplicity.
