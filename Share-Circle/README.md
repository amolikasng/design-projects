# Share Circle — App Design

An app concept that makes sharing subscriptions and splitting costs simpler and more convenient.

---

### The Concept

Share Circle is designed around the idea of making digital subscriptions more accessible through shared plans. The app allows users to connect with others, share subscription costs and manage their shared subscriptions in one place.

---

### The Design Direction

The interface focuses on simplicity, clarity and ease of use. A structured layout, intuitive navigation and clear information hierarchy help users understand their subscriptions, track shared expenses and manage their groups with minimal effort.
